# Safety

*Read before working on or near the illumination path.*

- Near-UV light can damage eyes and skin, including reflections and stray light from the beam splitter and objective.
- **A generic UV filter is not sufficient protection.** Any human viewing (eyepiece) path needs properly specified UV
  protection, and preferably physical shutters/interlocks so nobody can be exposed during exposure.
- Enclose the optical path and exposure area; interlock the enclosure where practical.
- Wear appropriate UV-rated eye protection when the source is on with the enclosure open.
- Photoresist, developer and cleaning chemicals: follow the lab's SOPs for handling, ventilation and waste.
